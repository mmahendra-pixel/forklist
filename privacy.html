Forklist

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short: Forklist does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. What you put in the app stays on your iPhone and, if you choose to share it, in your own iCloud with the family members you invite. We, the makers of Forklist, cannot see any of it.

What the app stores

Forklist keeps the things you add so it can work as your family's meal planner and shopping list:

  • your weekly menus, dish suggestions, recipes and chapati counts;
  • your shopping lists, prices, the shops you visit and what you spent;
  • the first names you give the family members in the app;
  • photos of dishes you choose to add to the family gallery, with their ratings;
  • pictures or PDFs of shopping bills you choose to add.

All of this is stored on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Family sharing through iCloud

If you turn on family sharing, the app uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to keep the family's phones in step. The data is saved in your own iCloud account and shared only with the people you invite. Apple stores it under the Apple Privacy Policy. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera and your photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo of a dish or a shopping bill. Bills are read on the iPhone itself, using Apple's built in text recognition. Nothing is uploaded for reading. The app only sees the photos you pick.

Notifications

The app can show notifications such as "the menu is published" or "Dad is doing the Lidl run". These come from your family's own activity through iCloud. They contain no advertising and are not used to track you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app contains no third party software that does.
  • We do not run servers that receive your data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Forklist is made for families, including children who use the treats and recipes. Because the app collects no personal data, no information about children is collected or shared. A parent controls family sharing and can choose which recipes need a grown up's OK.

Deleting your data

To remove everything from a phone, delete the app. To remove the shared family data from iCloud, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), choose Forklist and delete its data. You can also stop sharing at any time from the app's settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
