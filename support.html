Forklist

Support

Forklist plans the family's meals and turns them into a shopping list, one list per shop, with prices. Scan your bills to see what you spend.

Need help? Email forklist@icloud.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I share the app with my family?

Open the gear at the top right, tap Family sharing and send the invitation. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I change how much of an item to buy?

On Shop, tap the amount under the item's name. Choose a quick amount or use plus and minus.

The bill picked the wrong item. How do I fix it?

On the bill review screen, tap the item under the line and search for the right one. The app remembers your choice for the next bill.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

Is there a guide?

Yes. Open the gear and tap How to. Every screen is explained step by step.
